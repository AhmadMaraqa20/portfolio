Dublejli — AI Video Dubbing System

Speech-to-TextTranslationText-to-Speech
Exhibited +
🎥 Add a before/after clip showing original vs. dubbed video
Problem

Dubbing video content into another language typically needs a manual, multi-step production pipeline — transcription, translation, voice recording, and re-syncing.

Solution

An end-to-end AI pipeline that transcribes the original audio (STT), translates the text, generates new speech (TTS), and re-synchronizes the dubbed audio to match the original video's timing.

Implementation

Speech-to-Text → Text-to-Text translation → Text-to-Speech, with a custom synchronization algorithm to align generated speech duration with the original video. Exhibited at SOFEX Exhibition & Conference 2024 and the National Technology Parade (NTP) 2024.
