about

Computer engineer by training, AI engineer by trade.

How the signal-processing background feeds into the AI work.

Background

I'm a Computer Engineering graduate from the University of Jordan (GPA 3.77, Excellent), currently working as an AI Research & Integration Engineer at Penguin Location Services in Amman. My path into AI started from an unusual angle: real-time locating systems (RTLS) and BLE signal detection — figuring out where a physical object is, in real time, from noisy sensor data.

That systems-and-signals foundation now shows up in how I build AI products: I care about what happens when input is messy, when a model has to run locally, and when a workflow has to keep working in production — not just in a notebook.

What I do today

Day to day I work across large language models (RAG, embeddings, vector databases, local LLMs via Ollama), computer vision, and AI automation — building assistants and production workflows that connect language models to databases, APIs, and messaging platforms like WhatsApp Business API.

Outside of work

I like keeping a wide engineering base under the AI work — I still enjoy hardware and embedded projects (see the "Other Engineering" section on the Projects page for the RISC-V pipeline and PIC microcontroller work from my degree).
